# Provenance and reuse

Source: https://github.com/macs3-project/MACS/tree/v3.0.5/test at commit c5443190e3edfeb301cc94acf450e2b2c026a223. All source files are distributed in the public upstream MACS repository as integration-test fixtures. MACS source is BSD-3-Clause; upstream does not provide a separate per-fixture data licence. We preserve source names, repository/tag/commit, checksums and transformations, and do not infer patient identity or claim an additional dataset licence.

CTCF alignments retain upstream human chr22 coordinates; they have not been anonymised or synthetically regenerated. Yeast ATAC is identified upstream as SRR1822137. Variant BAM subsets are interval selections of public upstream alignments; no new alleles are fabricated. Synthetic tracks are clearly separated by the artificial chromosome name chrTest.
