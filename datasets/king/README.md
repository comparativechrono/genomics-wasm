# Synthetic KING inputs

These data are generated by `scripts/generate-king-fixtures.py` for portability
and regression testing. They contain no human genotypes or sample identifiers.

`cohort.bed`, `cohort.bim`, and `cohort.fam` form the main 105-sample PLINK dataset.
`small/` contains its first 25 samples. Both have 32,809 synthetic SNPs spanning
four chromosomes. `truth.json` records direct genotype counts and the expected
relationships; `manifest.json` pins every fixture by byte count and SHA-256.

The FAM pedigree declares two parents and two children. `COPY/DUP` has the same
genotypes as `FAM/CHILD1`, while the remaining samples are independent generated
founders. `U20` and `U100` have approximately 20% missing genotypes; `U100` is
absent from the smaller subset. A1 is the minor allele. The transmitted parental
haplotypes change over roughly 10 Mb blocks to exercise IBD segment inference.

The compressed FAM fixture is a supported-input test. Use plain-text BIM files;
KING's single-file BIM reader does not correctly parse a compressed BIM.

Regenerate with:

```sh
python3 scripts/generate-king-fixtures.py
```

See `validation/king/README.md` for tested commands and limitations.
