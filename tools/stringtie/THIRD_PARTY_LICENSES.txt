StringTie 3.0.3 browser package third-party notices

The complete pinned upstream offline archive is supplied with this package.
It contains gclib, the StringTie bundled HTSlib1.21, htscodecs1.4.0,
libdeflate1.23, bzip2 1.0.8 and XZ5.4.7. Zlib1.3.1 is supplied separately.
The optional SuperReads_RNA pipeline is not linked into this module.
