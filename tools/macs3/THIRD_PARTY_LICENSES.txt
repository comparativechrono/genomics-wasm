MACS3 WebAssembly third-party licences
===================================

MACS3 3.0.5: BSD-3-Clause; full text in LICENSE and scientific-notices/macs3/.
The incorporated fermi-lite/ksw and swalign sources carry MIT notices; SIMDe
includes MIT and CC0 notices. All extracted embedded notices are preserved.

Scientific dependencies (the exact versions and original archives are pinned
in sources.lock.json):
  NumPy, SciPy, pandas, scikit-learn, hmmlearn, h5py, anndata, joblib,
  threadpoolctl, array-api-compat, packaging, python-dateutil, pytz, six,
  natsort, pkgconfig and tzdata.

Their full original licence files and incorporated-code notices are in
scientific-notices/<component>/ and scientific-notices/<component>-wheel/.
This includes the alternatives in packaging, the CloudPickle notice in
joblib, and the upstream licences for the random generators, numerical
algorithms and other incorporated libraries. The files, not this index,
provide the complete terms and copyright attributions.

Compiled scientific dependencies also include OpenBLAS 0.3.31, its LAPACK and
libf2c (CLAPACK 3.2.1) code, HDF5 1.12.1, scientific zlib 1.3.1, and Boost
1.84.0. Their original source archives and full licence notices accompany the
package. pybind11 3.0.1 headers are used to compile hmmlearn; Cython 3.3.0
notices are in cython-notices/. Cython-generated code and pybind11 licences
remain separate from the Python runtime licence.

Core Python 3.14.2/Pyodide 314.0.7/Emscripten 5.0.3 and linked-library notices
are in runtime-notices/. Source locations, exact archive hashes and versions
are listed in runtime-sources.json and sources.lock.json. Scientific recipes
and patches are included as pyodide-recipes release 314-20260911 (MPL-2.0).

scientific-notices/manifest.json and runtime-notices/manifest.json identify
where every extracted notice came from and its SHA-256 checksum. The standalone
ZIP contains the corresponding original source archives under dist/macs3/sources/.
The complete archives preserve remaining per-file notices. Source archives can
include optional components; retaining their notices does not imply linkage.
