Emscripten 3.1.64 and its generated JavaScript/runtime components
Notices: LICENSE.emscripten. The upstream MIT/NCSA dual license and component
notices are reproduced there. The full SDK is a build prerequisite, not a
source archive redistributed with this tool.

The exact source archives and their SHA-256 checksums are listed in
sources.lock.json. The distribution carries these under sources/. All source
notices in those archives are retained. browser.patch and samtools.patch contain
the limited input-validation changes applied to the patched-native and WebAssembly builds;
the native baseline uses the upstream program source without those changes.
