ordinary non-overlapping string appends. These are output formatting changes;
the original gene prediction and training algorithms remain intact. This
modification is recorded in browser.patch, dated 12 October 2026. The unchanged
source archive, patch, build script and build instructions accompany the port.

original Emscripten licence notice in LICENSE.emscripten. No external gene
